Little plant secrets

Did you know?

Sampaguita

It has been the national flower of the Philippines since 1934.

Bougainvillea

Those bright "petals" are really colored leaves. The true flowers are the tiny white ones inside.

Snake plant

Unlike most plants, it takes in carbon dioxide at night, which is why it's a bedroom favorite.

Monstera

The holes in its leaves are natural. They appear as the plant grows up.
